# E-COMMERCE-SALES-AND-PROFIT-DASHBOARD
Built in Excel with Power Query, Power Pivot, PivotTables, PivotCharts and slicers

Overview
An interactive dashboard that turns raw e-commerce order data into clear sales and profit insights. It covers $37.17M in sales, $15.57M in profit and 29,627 orders, at a 42% profit margin.

The Question
On the surface, this is a healthy business. The goal was to find out what sits behind the totals and which areas need a closer look.

How It Was Built
Power Query cleaned the data. Power Pivot connected the tables into a data model. PivotTables and PivotCharts summarized performance, and slicers let users filter by year, month, region and product.

What the Data Shows

Sales go up and down with no steady growth.
Yearly sales move around $7.4M, while profit margin drifts down year over year.

November and December carry the year.
Together they bring in about $9.4M, while every other month sits between $2M and $3.3M.

The biggest category is not the most profitable.
Electronics leads on sales, but its 34% margin is below the 43% average. Grocery earns 55%.

Grocery is the best place to grow.
It is one of the smallest categories at $2.99M, yet it has the highest margin.

Outcome
The dashboard lets users move from headline totals to category and product detail without changing the underlying data. It also includes a recommendations section that turns the findings into next steps.

The most useful part isn't the charts. It's moving from "what happened" to "what should we do next."

Tools: Excel, Power Query, Power Pivot, PivotTables, PivotCharts, Slicers
